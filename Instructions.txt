

AMIGA CONTROLS:

Joystick or joypad in port 1 or keyboard

- Insert Coin: 5 key or Fire button (from menu).
- Start Game: 1 key or Up.
- Move: Joystick or Direction Arrows.
- Jumo: Ctrl or Fire button.
- Pause: P key.
- Quit & Save High-Scores (when run from AmigaDOS): ESC key.


USAGE:

Command line arguments (no WHDLoad)

donkey_kong INVINCIBLE/S,INFLIVES/S,INFTIME/S,CHEATKEYS/S,STARTLIVES/K/N


CHEATS:

INVINCIBLE: can't die.
INFLIVES: infinite lives.
INFTIME: infinite time.
CHEATKEYS:
 * F1: complete level.
 * F2: get hammer.
STARTLIVES: from 3 to 6.

If any cheat is selected, high scores won't be saved.
